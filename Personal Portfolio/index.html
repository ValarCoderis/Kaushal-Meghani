03

SolarTrack — Dual-Axis Solar Tracker

Physical Hardware Prototype
solartrack / hardware-prototype.png

A physical dual-axis solar tracking prototype developed to explore how a solar panel can adjust its orientation toward a stronger light source. The model integrates an Arduino Uno, LDR light sensors, servo motors, a small solar panel, and an LCD display. The project focuses on light detection, two-axis positioning, circuit integration, embedded control, and practical hardware testing.

KEY FEATURES Dual-axis solar panel positioning • LDR-based light detection • Arduino Uno control • Servo motor actuation • LCD output display • Physical prototype development and testing
HARDWARE Arduino Uno MCU, LDR sensor array, dual servo motors, mini solar panel, 16x2 LCD display
FOCUS Light detection, two-axis positioning, circuit integration, embedded control, and practical hardware testing
Dual-Axis Positioning LDR Light Sensors Arduino Uno Servo Motors LCD Output Display Physical Prototype Testing
